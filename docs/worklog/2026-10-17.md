## ASUS read-only SSD compatibility inventory

- One ext4 mount was identified for each managed SSD filesystem in the scoped inventory. The process metadata and nested open-file descriptors were examined with **read-only inspection commands**; the results do not establish that the processes themselves were read-only or exclude future and indirect writers.
- Found **12 first-level files with mode `0666` and 3 first-level directories with mode `0777`** across the two SSD partitions. These remain a separately scoped risk assessment under [Issue #263](https://github.com/wojko6/Advanced-ASUS-Edge-Gateway-ZTNA-Infrastructure/issues/263); tightening only mount-root permissions does not resolve them.
- Static source inspection of JFFS/Entware initialization found no direct mount-root write command in the matched literal path lines. This does **not** exclude shell indirection, aliases, firmware writers, Cron, concurrent processes or future startup changes.
