## Open gates / release decision

1. Private partition UUID binding and genuine media provenance, not merely `/dev/*` source appearance.
2. Read-only compatibility investigation for ASUS Linux 4.1.52 and real ext4 mount identity; QEMU cannot establish either.
3. Continuous mount/parent lifetime, concurrent writer exclusion and safe interrupted-operation / power-loss rollback.
4. Full storage consumer compatibility, swap-before-AMTM boot order, precise post-mount hook integration, separately authorized migrator design and reboot persistence.
5. Remain on stable DUFS; do not deploy an RC, change production mount modes or merge experimental security repairs without separate approval.
