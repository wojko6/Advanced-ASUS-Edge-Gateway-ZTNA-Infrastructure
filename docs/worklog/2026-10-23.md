## 1. Original issue versus laboratory research

Issue #287 was opened following a static audit of the pinned `native_bounded_exec()` worker/timer lifecycle. Its acceptance criteria concern orphaned external `sleep`, timeout/cancellation races, repeated and concurrent invocations, child lifetime and FD9 closure, and—if needed—a BusyBox 1.25.1-compatible deterministic cleanup. **A kill of an unrelated process by PID reuse has not been demonstrated.**

Existing Fedora mock, process-group, seccomp and ARMv7/QEMU studies explored an alternative supervisor. They remain **off-router**, with router Linux 4.1.52 seccomp support unverified. They are **not** a production fix, nor a reason to expand the original Issue acceptance implicitly.
