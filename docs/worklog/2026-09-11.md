## SSD and Entware migration

The router's persistent Entware environment was migrated from the previous USB flash-drive deployment to a dedicated Crucial MX500 M.2 SATA SSD in a USB enclosure.

The SSD is separated into two filesystems:

- `ENTWARE` — dedicated to Entware and router-side services.
- `ROUTER_DATA` — dedicated to data and future storage workflows, isolated from Entware service files.

After migration, a controlled reboot confirmed automatic mounting of both filesystems, correct `/opt` availability, and swap activation. Two swap files were active after boot: approximately 512 MiB on `ENTWARE` and 2 GiB on `ROUTER_DATA`, with no swap use observed during the validation snapshot.

Detailed migration and recovery notes are documented in [`../ENTWARE-SSD-MIGRATION.md`](../ENTWARE-SSD-MIGRATION.md). Sanitized validation evidence is retained under [`../../evidence/2026-09-11/`](../../evidence/2026-09-11/).

## Adaptive QoS boot failure

Fresh and historical boot logs exposed repeatable ASUS Adaptive QoS errors, including failures associated with QoS registration and activation.

Adaptive QoS was disabled through the router configuration interface and the resulting state was verified with `qos_enable=0`. A subsequent controlled reboot no longer produced the recurring QoS failure pattern.

The final post-reboot critical-log check showed no fresh project `ERROR`, out-of-memory, fatal-error, or recurring QoS-failure entries in the validation window.
