# Evidence integrity note — 2026-09-22

## Scope

The 2026-09-21 independent audit identified that the historical bundles dated
2026-09-03, 2026-09-04, 2026-09-05, 2026-09-08 and 2026-09-09 no longer pass a
full `sha256sum -c SHA256SUMS` because their checked-in `README.md` files were
edited after the original manifests were generated.

The other files covered by those historical manifests were reported by the audit
as matching their recorded hashes. This note does **not** rewrite any historical
checksum and does not present the mismatch as evidence of tampering.

## Provenance policy

Historical manifests remain unchanged so Git history preserves the original
record. CI treats the five known README mismatches as legacy exceptions and still
verifies every other manifest entry.

For evidence generated by the current collector:

- `REPORT.txt` is the immutable provenance record included in `SHA256SUMS`;
- raw/sanitized evidence outputs remain covered by `SHA256SUMS`;
- `README.md` is an explicitly mutable narrative and is intentionally excluded
  from the immutable manifest;
- a future evidence directory with `SHA256SUMS` must pass
  `sha256sum -c SHA256SUMS` in full unless it is one of the five documented
  historical legacy bundles above.

This separates integrity of collected evidence from later editorial clarification
of its interpretation.
